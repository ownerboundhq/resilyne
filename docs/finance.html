What works today

The local pilot reconciles CSV pairs and calculates specialist month-end reports, including FIFO inventory, payroll and GST/HST tie-outs, accruals, prepaids and depreciation. A restricted native Windows input demo types the approved practice close adjustments into its own window and verifies saved results. It uses deterministic rules, not a general-purpose AI operator. No live accounting integrations, tax filing, payments, or professional assurance are enabled. Do not upload financial data to this public site.

Connect your existing software

Four test-only read adapters are implemented: Google Sheets, Shopify, Stripe and QuickBooks Online sandbox. Excel has a separate local export pilot. All other connections below are planned; live account automation is not available.
